Buyer FAQ

Answers before the buyer asks.

This page keeps the sales story grounded: practical AI integration, no fake guarantees, and small proof before deep automation.

Is this just a booking website?

No. The booking/intake page is Level 1: AI-ready intake. It creates immediate customer friction reduction and cleaner service-request data for later AI chat, searchable business database, or workflow automation.

Is this AI or not?

It is practical AI integration in levels. Some businesses should start with structured intake before AI chat. Others are ready for Website AI Chat or an AI-Searchable Business Brain.

Will this guarantee leads?

No guaranteed leads. No guaranteed Google rankings. No ad performance claim. The truthful claim is clearer customer action, cleaner intake data, and a staged path toward useful AI.

Can you add a chatbot?

Yes: Website AI Chat is a separate tier. It should be controlled, scoped to real business facts, logged, and connected to lead handoff rather than allowed to answer anything.

Can you make our files searchable?

Yes: AI-Searchable Business Brain ingests docs, SOPs, services, policies, and estimate references into source-grounded search with citations.

Can AI automate follow-up or quotes?

Yes, but only later: Workflow Automation needs approval gates, audit logs, bounded tasks, and access/safety review before any live customer system integration.

Who approves public messages and price/scope?

The business approves pricing presentation, scope, customer-system access, and any public/live deployment before those steps happen.

Non-negotiables

No guaranteed leads. No guaranteed Google rankings. No fake reviews. No customer-system access without scope. No deep automation without approval gates and business approval required.

Where does a demo booking go?

For pre-sale demos, booking requests are saved locally in the page/admin preview. Live customer delivery is configured only after the business approves scope and setup. No hidden customer-system integration is promised.
