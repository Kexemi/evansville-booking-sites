Thought partner

Individuals use generic AI for drafts, summaries, ideas, and quick analysis.

  • Low coordination
  • No source control
  • No business memory

Controlled assistant

  • FAQ assistant

  • Human approvals
  • Metrics
  • Repeatable loops
