📋 Allied Electrical Contractors — Review Request Playbook

How and when to ask customers for Google Reviews — and build a 5-star reputation.

Why Reviews Matter

Allied Electrical currently has 0 reviews on Yelp and needs a strong Google presence. A steady flow of Google reviews builds trust, improves local search ranking, and directly wins more jobs. Our target is 50+ Google reviews at a 4.5★ average.

⚡ Key Stat: SMS review requests have a ~3× higher open rate than email. Prefer texting when you have the customer's mobile number.

The Workflow

  1. Complete the job — do excellent work, clean up, and thank the customer.
  2. Open this system (bookmark it on your phone) and enter the customer's details.
  3. Copy the SMS (preferred) or email template generated for you.
  4. Send within 2 hours of finishing the job — conversion drops sharply after that.
  5. Click "Mark as Sent" to log it in the tracker.
  6. Follow up if no review appears within 3–5 days (use the follow-up template).
  7. Confirm the review appears on Google and update the status in the system.

When to Ask — The Golden Moment

The best time to ask for a review is immediately after positive feedback — right when the customer says "thank you" or compliments your work. Strike while the feeling of satisfaction is fresh.

  • Right after payment — "By the way, I'm glad everything looks great. Would you mind leaving a quick Google review? It helps us a ton."
  • During the final walk-through — when you show them the finished work and they're impressed.
  • Within 2 hours of leaving — send the SMS generated by this system.
  • Next-day follow-up — a friendly text or email if you haven't seen a review appear.

Never Do This

  • ❌ Don't offer discounts or payments for reviews — that violates Google's policies and can get reviews removed.
  • ❌ Don't ask unhappy customers for a review. Fix the issue first, then ask.
  • ❌ Don't write fake reviews — Google catches these and it damages your reputation.
  • ❌ Don't ask more than twice — one request + one follow-up is plenty.
  • ❌ Don't wait longer than 48 hours — the warm feeling fades fast.

Tracking & Accountability

  • Use the Dashboard tab to see total requests, conversion rate, and streak.
  • Use the History tab to see every request logged with dates.
  • Update a request's status to "Reviewed ✓" once you confirm the review is live on Google.
  • Aim for at least 1 request per working day to build momentum.

Estimated Milestones

  • 10 reviews — Your Google star rating appears prominently in search results.
  • 25 reviews — Strong social proof; most competitors at this level.
  • 50 reviews — 🎯 TARGET — top-tier presence for Evansville electrical contractors.
